# SocialDesk — One-Page Guide

*The whole system on one page. Everything else is detail.*

**What it is.** One inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger. Customers message you; your
team replies from a single screen, with contacts, saved audiences, campaigns and automatic
replies built in.

---

## The one rule that shapes everything

You can write free-form text **only within 24 hours** of the customer's last message. After
that you must send a **pre-approved template**. Every new customer message re-opens the window.
This is Meta's rule, not ours — the app blocks the send and tells you why.

## Your daily loop

1. **Inbox** — answer conversations.
2. **Templates** — reach people whose 24-hour window has closed.
3. **Campaigns** — send one template to a saved **Audience**.

## The five words you'll meet

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Live conversations |
| **Audiences** | Saved groups of contacts, filtered by tag, stage, channel, city or last interaction |
| **Templates** | Pre-approved messages Meta lets you send outside the 24-hour window |
| **Campaigns** | A template + an audience + a schedule |
| **Automations** | Auto-reply to a keyword, route a new chat to the right person, follow up when a chat goes quiet |

Also in the sidebar: **Lead CRM** (contacts + pipeline stages), **Analytics**, **Billing**
(the wallet), **Channels** (connected numbers), **Team**.

## What the wallet pays for

| Send | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Free-form reply inside the 24-hour window | Free |
| Marketing template | 88 paise |
| Utility or authentication template | 30 paise |

The wallet warns at **₹50**. If it can't cover a send, the message is refused rather than
half-sent — and a permanent delivery failure is refunded back to the wallet.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including connecting channels, billing and the Team screen |
| **Admin** | Everything except owner-only actions; can manage agents, not other admins |
| **Agent** | Day-to-day work: inbox, contacts, campaigns, templates, audiences, automations. No Team screen |

The owner can never be removed or demoted.

## Set-up checklist (once)

1. **Channels** — connect your WhatsApp number, Instagram and/or Facebook Page.
2. **Templates** — get them approved in Meta Business Manager, then press **Sync from Meta**.
3. **Team** — invite your staff; they accept from the email link.
4. **Billing** — top up the wallet.
5. **Automations** *(optional)* — set business hours, auto-replies and routing.

## Five things worth knowing

1. Incoming messages appear in **a second or two**, and outgoing messages leave just as fast
   while the background worker is running (a cron job keeps it alive).
2. **Template approval is Meta's call** — usually minutes, occasionally up to 24 hours.
3. **Self-service WhatsApp connection** needs Meta **Business Verification + App Review**.
   Until that's approved, channels are connected by pasting a token from Meta Business Manager
   — same end result, more clicking.
4. **Instagram and Messenger** allow free-form replies only inside the 24-hour window, exactly
   like WhatsApp.
5. **Invite emails currently land in the server log**, not a real inbox — share the invite link
   directly until that's switched on.

## If something looks wrong

1. **Refresh** — the inbox is live, so a stale tab is the usual culprit.
2. **Check Billing** — a low wallet is the most common cause of a refused send.
3. **Send us the screenshot** and the customer's number.
